What is wrong with the builder, and five ways to fix each part

I measured every control in the running app at 1600, 1280 and 390 px. Below: what is broken, the fixes I will make whatever you choose, then five decisions with five live options each. Every demo is clickable. The recommended option is marked.

Audit

What the measurements show

  1. HighNo control systemOne toolbar mixes four heights, three text sizes and four corner shapes. 44 px52 px70 px100 px12 / 14 / 16 px text0 / 9.6 / 12 px / pill
  2. HighFalcon pictures inflate the toolbarThe sample bird in Design and Labels makes those cards 70 and 100 px tall, so the toolbar needs three rows at 1280 px.
  3. HighYour chosen birds are out of reachThe "Your poster" tray and the presets sit under the bird list, which is now 1,161 birds and about 51,000 px long. Nobody scrolls that far.
  4. High"Add all 1,161" is offeredA poster holds at most 150 birds, so this button can only make a mess. It is also as big as the search field.
  5. MediumTap bar looks weakThe mirror icon is unclear at 20 px, the bar covers the neighbouring birds, and the green outline matches nothing else.
  6. MediumSwap has no motionDropping a bird on another makes both jump to their new places in one frame.
  7. MediumSmall defectsBase buttons animate every property, disabled buttons ignore the pointer, and the background picker repeats the selected name under the swatches.
Will do

Fixes I will make whatever you pick

  • One spacing and type scaleSpacing 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32 px. Text 12, 14, 16, 20, 28 px. Nothing outside them.
  • No falcon picturesDesign and Labels become plain text choices, the same height as every other control.
  • Tap a bird to mirror itNo more bar and no Remove button. A tap turns the bird, with the animation you pick in D. Keyboard users keep Delete.
  • Remove birds where you chose themUntick a card in the list, or use the chosen birds view you pick in C.
  • Presets above the list"Start from a set" becomes a menu next to the search, not a row under 51,000 px of birds.
  • "Add all" only when it makes senseA small button, shown for a family or a search with 150 birds or fewer.
Decision A

Where the style controls live on desktop

Shown at a real 1280 x 720 desktop, scaled to fit. The poster is limited by height, so width spent on controls beside it is almost free, while every row above it shrinks it.

Decision B

One shape for every button and control

The same set of controls in each system. Whatever you pick, every control in the app uses it, with one size and one small size.

Decision C

Seeing and unticking your chosen birds

Try it: click a chosen bird to take it off the poster. Click the stage background to bring them all back.

Decision D

The mirror animation when you tap a bird

Tap any bird to mirror it. Each stays under half a second, and people who ask for reduced motion get an instant turn.

Decision E

The swap animation when you drop a bird on another

Press Play to watch the barn owl dragged onto the kingfisher.
